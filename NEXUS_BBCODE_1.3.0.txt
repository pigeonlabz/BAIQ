[size=5][b]Backpack & Inventory QoL 1.3.0[/b][/size]

[b]By PigeonLabs[/b]

Thank you for 5,000 unique downloads! Version 1.3.0 expands production options while preserving separate builds for Schedule I MAIN and BETA.

[b]What's new[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Increase Production:[/b] Optional, independent support for Drying Racks, Mixing Station V1, Mixing Station V2, and hand-harvested weed and coca. Each uses the selected Stack Size multiplier (2x, 4x, or 8x); processing and growth timing remain native.
[*][b]Cash quick-move:[/b] Shift-click cash between your inventory and supported general storage, including lockers, safes, racks, and closets. Machine inventories remain excluded from general cash routing.
[*][b]Delivery safety:[/b] Retains the item-loss protection for delivery stack chunking. Purchased delivery quantities are unchanged.
[*]Existing Backpack and inventory quality-of-life features remain available.
[/list]

[b]Compatibility[/b]
[list]
[*][b]MAIN:[/b] Schedule I 0.4.6f13 — `BackpackInventoryQoL.dll` version 1.3.0
[*][b]BETA:[/b] Schedule I 0.4.7 — `BackpackInventoryQoL.Beta047.dll` version 1.3.0-beta.47
[*]Install the DLL for your branch only; do not install both at once.
[/list]

[b]Known issue[/b]
Mixing Station V1's skillcheck display may not show both recipe ingredients clearly. Live checks confirmed the quantities and output behaved correctly.

[b]Not included in 1.3.0[/b]
Packaging Station scaling, Delivery Capacity, Mushroom Bed yield, and scaling for the Cauldron, Chemistry Station, Lab Oven, Brick Press, and Mushroom Spawn Station are deferred.
